# MKD Slidea — User Manual

**Interactive live presentations, polls, quizzes & word clouds — for teachers, trainers and event hosts.**
Platform: [slidea.mismath.net](https://slidea.mismath.net) · Version: July 2026

Welcome to **MKD Slidea**, the platform that turns any classroom, training or event into a two-way conversation. This manual walks you through everything — from your first login to running a live session with hundreds of participants responding from their phones.

> **How to read this manual:** Sections 2–6 are for **hosts** (the person running the session). Section 7 is for **participants** (the audience). Section 8 covers **templates & pricing**, and Section 9 gives **teaching tips** to get the most out of every session.

---

## 1. What is MKD Slidea?

MKD Slidea is a real-time audience-engagement platform. As a host, you project a presentation on a screen; your audience joins from any phone or laptop using a short code, and answers polls, quizzes, word clouds, ratings and open questions **live**. Their responses appear instantly on your screen as animated charts, leaderboards and word clouds.

**What you can do with it:**
- Run **8 types of activities**: multiple-choice poll, quiz (with leaderboard), word cloud, open text, star rating, ranking, 1–10 scale, and multi-question surveys.
- See **live results** projected to the room, with a real-time participant counter.
- Use your **phone as a remote control** while the presentation runs on the projector.
- Generate questions with **AI**, aligned to your curriculum.
- Reuse and share **ready-made templates**.
- Brand your sessions with your own colors and logo.

No app to install — participants just open a link or scan a QR code.

## 4. Creating Your First Event

From the Dashboard, click **New presentation** (or open an existing event). You enter the **Host view**, where you build and run your session.

### 4.1 The activity list
Every activity (question) you add appears as a card. Click a card to make it **active** — that's what your audience sees and responds to right now.

![Host activity list](images/16-host-activity-list.png)

*Each card is one activity. The active one is highlighted; click any card to put it on screen.*

### 4.2 Choosing an activity type
Click **Add activity** to see all 8 activity types. Pick the one that fits your goal.

![Activity type picker](images/13-host-activity-types.png)

*The eight activity types: poll, word cloud, quiz, open text, rating, ranking, 1–10 scale, and survey form.*

## 5. Adding Activities

### 5.1 A poll (multiple choice)
Type your question and the answer options, then **Save activity**.

![Create a poll](images/14-host-create-poll.png)

### 5.2 A quiz (with a correct answer)
Same as a poll, but mark the correct option with the ✓ button. Quizzes give participants instant feedback and build a leaderboard.

![Create a quiz](images/15-host-create-quiz.png)

### 5.3 Other types
- **Word cloud** — participants submit single words; the most common grow largest on screen. Great for warm-ups and brainstorming.
- **Open text** — free-form answers shown as sticky notes.
- **Rating** — a 1–5 star rating for quick pulse checks.
- **Ranking** — participants order items by importance (the whole order counts, not just the top pick).
- **Scale 1–10** — a slider-style agreement scale.
- **Survey** — several sub-questions at once (open, scale, or choice).

> **Tip:** You can attach an image to any activity, and let AI suggest or generate the question for you.

## 7. Going Live — The Presenter View

Click **Presentation** (or open `/event/<code>/present`) to show your session on the big screen. As people respond, the results animate live.

![Presenter with live results](images/21-presenter-results.png)

*The presenter view: the live question, an animated bar chart of responses (the leading option wears a 👑), the participant counter ("2 live", "1/2 answered"), and a chart-mode switcher (Bars / Donut / Podium / Numbers). A QR code and the join code are always visible.*

You can switch chart styles, **pause** voting, run a **timer**, or fire **confetti** to celebrate a result. For quizzes, a **leaderboard** appears on the right.

### 7.1 The Remote Control
While the presentation runs on the projector, open the host page on your **phone** and tap **Remote**. Your phone becomes a clicker — advance questions, lock voting and reset with big, one-tap buttons.

![Remote control on phone](images/22-remote-control.png)

*The phone remote: the active question, response progress, and large previous/next, lock and reset controls.*

## 10. Teaching Tips — Getting the Most Out of a Session

These practices come from classroom and training experience:

1. **Start with a low-stakes warm-up.** A word cloud or a fun poll in the first 60 seconds gets everyone to join and tap — once they've interacted once, they keep engaging.
2. **One question, one idea.** Keep each activity focused on a single concept so the results are easy to read and discuss.
3. **Use the result as a teaching moment.** Pause on a surprising chart and ask *"Why do you think most of you chose this?"* — the data becomes a discussion starter.
4. **Mix activity types.** Alternate quick polls with a quiz (energy + competition) and an open question (depth + reflection) to keep the rhythm varied.
5. **Ranking reveals priorities.** When you want to know *what matters most*, ranking gives a richer picture than a single-choice poll.
6. **Lock before you discuss.** Lock voting before revealing the "right" answer in a quiz so late votes don't skew the result.
7. **Project the word cloud.** Watching their words grow on screen is highly motivating for shy participants.
8. **Use the timer** for timed challenges and to keep the session on schedule.
9. **End with a rating or open reflection** — *"One thing I learned today…"* — to close the loop and capture feedback.
10. **Save as a template.** A session that worked well is worth reusing; publish it so colleagues benefit too.

## 11. Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **Participants can't join?** Check the code on the presenter screen matches what they typed; make sure the session isn't paused or ended.
- **No results appearing?** Confirm the activity is **active** (highlighted in the host list) and that the presenter tab is open.
- **Forgot your password?** Use *Forgot password?* on the sign-in dialog.
- **Need more than 200 participants?** Upgrade to Pro or an institutional plan.
- **Data & privacy:** MKD Slidea is GDPR-compliant; see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in the footer.
